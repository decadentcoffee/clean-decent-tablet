A cleaner experience for your Decent

Less software in the background. More like part of the machine.

Why we made this

While we were making Decadent, we wanted the cleanest overall experience for our coffee machine, so we decided to de-bloat the tablet and streamline the user interface.

This script is useful on its own so we're releasing the code and hosting it for use.

The clean-up and the new home screen work for any DE1 owner, and Decadent is one optional icon. The home screen runs Fossify Launcher, so you can customize it however you like afterwards.

What we keep, what we switch off, and why

The tablet's job is to run your DE1. Out of the box it also carries a phone's worth of apps and background services that have nothing to do with espresso, and many of them send data back to someone. So we keep what the DE1 and the tablet's safety need, and switch off the rest.

  • Fewer things phoning home. Games, shopping, sharing features and Samsung's data and advertising agents are switched off. Each one is a source of telemetry and a target for updates and pop-ups the machine never needed.
  • Keep updates and security patches. Samsung's firmware updater stays, so the tablet still gets Android security updates. That keeps a little of Samsung's diagnostics running, the price of patches.
  • One browser: Chrome. A web app like Decadent needs a browser that stays up to date. Android already runs on Google's browser engine and Google Play services, so Chrome adds no new company to the list. Samsung Internet would add another.
  • A Google account, any one. Signing in lets the Play Store keep Chrome and its engine updated, and lets Chrome install web apps such as Decadent full screen. We suggest a new account just for the tablet, because the tablet has no lock. One you already use works too.
  • Nothing deleted, everything reversible. Apps are switched off, not removed, and undo puts it all back.

Two choices, take either or both

  • Clean up: about 100 bundled apps and background services are switched off, not deleted, and any can be brought back later. Chrome becomes the browser, and the screen sleeps after 10 minutes.
  • New Home screen: large icons for the apps you use, Decaid and Decadent or the original Decent app, and swipe gestures so apps get the whole screen.

No changes will be made without your OK.

Before you start

You need

  • A computer with Chrome or Edge. On Windows, close Android Studio, Samsung Smart Switch and any other Android tool first: Windows lets only one program use the tablet.
  • A USB cable that carries data.
  • A Samsung tablet supplied by Decent Espresso: model SM-X133, shown in Settings → About tablet.

These changes aren't permanent and nothing is deleted, so you can try it and revert easily.

Rather set Decadent up by hand?

You don't need this page to use Decadent. On the tablet:

  1. Install Decaid from its releases page, open it and answer its first questions.
  2. In Decaid open Plugins, tap the cog, then Install plugin, then GitHub Release. Type decadentcoffee/decadent-plugin and tap Install. Then on Decadent in that list, open the ⋮ menu and tap Load, and switch on Auto-load on startup.
  3. In Chrome open decadentcoffee.app/app and log in. The tablet links to your account by itself.

The clean-up and the Home screen can be done by hand too: the full written guide.

Let the tablet talk to this computer

On the tablet:

  1. Open Settings → About tablet → Software information.
  2. Tap Build number seven times, until it says Developer options are on.
  3. Go back to Settings and open Developer options. If you can't see it, it's at the bottom of Settings.
  4. Turn on USB debugging, and tap OK.
  5. Plug the tablet into this computer, and leave it unlocked.

Connect the tablet

  1. Your browser lists USB devices. Pick your tablet (it may say SAMSUNG or SM-X133) and choose Connect.
  2. On the tablet, tick Always allow from this computer, then tap Allow.

Nothing changes until you choose. Next you pick what to change.

Look at the tablet: tick Always allow from this computer, then tap Allow. Without the tick it asks again when it restarts. Waiting for you…

      What to do next
      
  
      A few tips for everyday use, and how to make the tablet your own.
      
  
      Everyday use
      
  
        
    
      • Home: swipe up from the very bottom edge.
        • 
    
      • All apps: on Home, swipe up from the middle of the screen.
        • 
    
      • Wake: double-tap the screen, or press the power button.
        • 
    
      • After a restart: open Decaid once, then Decadent.
        • 
    
      • Keep Decaid running: don't use Close all in the recent apps view. It stops Decaid, and the machine loses its connection.
        • 
  
      
  
      Change the icons on Home
      
  
        
    
      • Add an app: open all apps, touch and hold the app, and drag it to an empty space on Home.
        • 
    
      • Move an icon: touch and hold it, then drag it.
        • 
    
      • Take an icon off Home: touch and hold it and choose Remove. The app stays installed and is still in all apps.
        • 
    
      • Grid, icon size and more: touch and hold an empty space on Home and choose Launcher settings. The Home screen is Fossify Launcher, so all its options are yours to change.
        • 
  
      
  
      Want an app back?
      
  
        
    
      • Nothing was deleted. Open Settings → Apps, use the filter to show disabled apps, open the app and tap Enable. You can also find it in the Play Store and tap Enable.
        • 
    
      • Apps you install from the Play Store work as normal. Touch and hold one in all apps to put it on Home.
        • 
    
      • To put everything back as it was, come back to this page and connect the tablet: Turn apps back on and Put the original Home screen back appear among the choices.
        •
